# Stylemeter bonuses

Stylemeter has 254 named bonuses in 14 sections. They are listed here in the same order as in the settings panel.

- The points are the base, before the multipliers for thread tension, height, bosses and the points multiplier from
  the menu. How they combine is in [MECHANICS.md](MECHANICS.md).
- The names stay in English in the game, whatever language it runs in.
- Every bonus can be switched off in **Options > Stylemeter**, one by one or a whole section at once. A bonus that is
  off gives no points and no line in the feed.
- "During a combo" means while the meter is running: from the first points until it drops below DREAMER.
- "Without landing" means in one go between leaving the ground and touching it again. Clinging to a wall and bouncing
  off things are not landings.
- Distances are in game units. The screen is about 30 units wide.
- Plain hits also score, silently, without a line in the feed. They are not bonuses and are not listed here: see
  [MECHANICS.md](MECHANICS.md#1-points-formula).

## 12. Bosses

Hits, clashes and other actions against a boss count x2. The result of a fight comes once, when the boss is beaten,
and is not doubled: the named win, plus FLAWLESS VICTORY, SHOWSTOPPER, JACKPOT!, GREAT ENEMY FELLED,
STUDENT BECOMES THE MASTER and UNFORGIVEN when they apply.
